Open the same note in a Relay Shared Folder to work together from your own vaults. You can ask a question beside a passage, reply to each other, or suggest new wording.

## Open the same note

If you already share a folder through Relay, open a Markdown note from that folder on both devices. To comment, reply, or suggest an edit, each person needs write access to the note and [Relay Comments enabled in their vault](/comments/install-relay-comments/).

If you’re setting this up for the first time, each person [installs Relay and signs in](/guides/install-relay-for-obsidian/#sign-in-to-relay). Then [share the folder](/guides/share-a-folder/) and [invite your collaborator](/guides/invite-a-collaborator/). After joining your Relay Server, your collaborator selects **Add to vault** for the Shared Folder. Once the folder has synced, you can both open the note.

## Discuss a passage

1. In Live Preview, select the passage and click **Add comment**. Write your question and click **Comment**.
2. In their copy of the note, your collaborator opens the thread, types an answer in **Reply…**, and clicks **Reply**. Their answer appears in the same thread in your vault.
3. When the discussion is finished, click **Resolve** on the thread in the review sidebar. The passage stays in the shared note.

## Review a suggested change

To offer new wording, select the text and run **Mark selection as substitution** from the command palette. Enter your wording and click **Insert substitution**. Your collaborator can hover over the suggestion and choose **Accept** to keep the new wording or **Reject** to keep the original. Relay syncs the resulting text to both vaults.

For suggestions with a discussion attached, see [Accept or reject a suggestion](/comments/suggest-edits/#accept-or-reject-a-suggestion).
