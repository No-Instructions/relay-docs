Relay settings is where you sign in, join or create a Relay Server, share folders, and manage who has access.

## On desktop

- Command palette: press **Cmd+P** on macOS or **Ctrl+P** on Windows and Linux, then run **Relay: Open settings**.
- Ribbon: click the **Relay** icon in the far-left ribbon.
- Settings: click the **Settings** gear at the bottom of the left sidebar, then select **Relay** under **Community plugins**.

## On mobile

- Command palette: pull down from the top of the app to open the command palette, then run **Relay: Open settings**. Pulling down runs Obsidian's quick action, which opens the command palette unless you've changed it.
- Menu: when you're not editing a note, tap the menu at the bottom-right of the navigation bar. Ribbon actions, including **Relay**, appear there.
- Settings: open the left sidebar, tap **Settings**, then select **Relay** under **Community plugins**.



If Relay isn't listed under **Community plugins**, or **Relay: Open settings** isn't in the command palette, Relay isn't installed or enabled. Install and enable Relay from Obsidian's **Community plugins** first.
